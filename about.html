01 — The Firm

Built for depth, not volume.

DinaBridge was built around a simple observation: most consulting firms are too broad to be useful when a production system is failing. They staff generalists, add management layers, and charge for coordination. The engineers who actually understand the system are rarely the ones in the room.

We built the opposite. Every engagement is led by a named senior engineer who works directly inside your environment. No project managers between you and the technical work. No junior resources ramping up on your timeline. Just the right expert, scoped to your exact problem.

Elasticsearch specialization is what makes this possible. Observability pipelines, relevance tuning, SIEM detection engineering, Kafka event streams, Kubernetes orchestration, ScienceLogic SL1 — each of these takes years of production experience to do well. Surface-level knowledge and certifications do not move a struggling cluster.

The result is a consultancy that operates more like a specialist practice than a staffing agency. Narrow by design. Senior by default. Accountable to outcomes.

03 — How We Work

The operating principles.

Every engagement is structured the same way: one named senior engineer, a defined scope, and a documented handoff. These are not aspirations — they are the conditions under which we take on work.

01 Named engineer, always Every engagement has one named senior engineer. You know who is doing the work. They are reachable directly. There is no rotation, no hand-off, no mystery about who owns what.
02 Scoped and bounded We define what we are doing, how long it will take, and what done looks like before we start. No open-ended retainers. No scope creep without explicit agreement.
03 Documented and handed off Every engagement ends with documentation your team owns. Runbooks, architecture decisions, configuration rationale. You should not need us again for the same problem.
04 Outcomes over hours We measure success by what is working in production, not by hours logged. Engagements are scoped to deliverables. Time is a budget constraint, not a billing mechanism.
05 No lock-in We do not build dependencies. Our goal is to leave your team more capable than we found it. If that means we are not needed again, that is a successful engagement.
06 Honest assessment first Advisory reviews are designed to give you an accurate picture before you commit to hands-on work. If the problem is not one we can solve, we will tell you that.
