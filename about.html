How we work

The operating principles.

Named engineers only

You know who’s doing the work before the engagement starts. The engineer on the call is the engineer in your cluster.

Scoped and documented

Every engagement has a defined scope, deliverables list, and written handoff. No open-ended retainers that drift.

Production-first thinking

We design for the environment your cluster actually runs in — not a clean-room demo setup. Real data volumes, real query patterns.

No fluff delivery

Deliverables are working configurations, runbooks, and architecture docs — not slide decks summarizing what you already told us.
