What we look for

  • 5+ years shipping production infrastructure in one of our practice areas (observability, search, security data)
  • At least one vendor certification on our roadmap — or the depth to earn one within your first quarter (we pay for it)
  • Comfortable writing runbooks and architecture docs your client can defend internally
  • Consulting temperament — you’d rather solve a customer’s problem than defend a slide deck
  • US-based (remote) with overlap for eastern & central time zones

How to apply

We don’t post req IDs. Send a short note about the last production system you fixed, the certification you hold, and what you’d want to work on next. A partner reads every application personally.

Internships

Your first step into corporate America.

We intentionally host interns from university programs and Virtual Internships. It’s how we support the field — and how we meet the specialists we’ll want to hire in three years.

University co-op & senior projects

We host interns from US universities that fund student placements or count the work toward a capstone / practicum credit. We help scope a shippable project up front and provide the mentor sign-offs your program requires.

Virtual Internships partnership

DinaBridge is a host company on the Virtual Internships platform. Structured 8–24 week remote placements with weekly check-ins, coaching, and a portfolio-ready deliverable. Ideal for international students and career changers entering US tech.

First step into corporate America

We’re deliberate about hiring people who’ve never worked inside a US tech company before. You’ll be paired with a senior engineer, sit in on real client work under NDA, and leave with a written recommendation and reference network.

What you’ll get

  • Real work on non-critical paths — no coffee runs, no busywork
  • Written weekly feedback and a mid-term review
  • One production-quality deliverable you can put in your portfolio
  • Access to our internal learning tracks (observability, search, security fundamentals)

For universities & programs

If your program funds student placements or wraps them into course credit, we’re set up to work with you. Email us with the program details and a typical cohort size.
