02 — How a Migration Works

Six phases. Every one documented.

We do not skip phases to ship faster. The technical migration is only one part — stakeholder enablement, runbook creation, and validation gates are built into the plan from the start.

Phase 01 01 Discovery & Inventory We map every data source, pipeline, alert rule, dashboard, and integration in your current environment. Nothing is assumed — we document what is actually running, not what the vendor told you was running.
Phase 02 02 Target Architecture Design We design the Elastic cluster architecture, index strategy, ILM policy, shard topology, and ingestion pipeline layout for your specific data volumes, retention requirements, and query patterns.
Phase 03 03 Content Conversion Detection rules, saved searches, dashboards, and queries are translated from the source platform’s syntax into Elastic equivalents. We use proven conversion tooling customized for your environment — not manual rewrites.
Phase 04 04 Parallel Running & Validation Before cutover, both systems run in parallel. We validate alert parity, data fidelity, and dashboard accuracy against defined acceptance criteria. Cutover only happens when the gate passes.
Phase 05 05 Cutover & Stabilisation We manage the production cutover, monitor cluster health in the critical first period, and address any issues that surface under real load. We stay engaged through stabilisation — not just until go-live.
Phase 06 06 Team Enablement & Handoff Your engineering and stakeholder teams are trained on the new environment. Every pipeline, rule, and dashboard is documented. We hand off capability — not a dependency on us.
