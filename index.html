03 — How We Engage

A short path from problem to production.

No discovery phases. No proposal decks. A senior engineer diagnoses the actual environment, designs the fix, implements it, and hands over with documentation.

01 Diagnose Read the cluster, query the telemetry, review the architecture. We find what is actually wrong before touching anything.
02 Design Target architecture with explicit latency, cost, and reliability budgets. Not a recommendation — a concrete plan with tradeoffs stated.
03 Implement Senior engineers implement in your production environment, shipping the change and monitoring the outcome.
04 Hand Over Your team owns it. Full documentation, runbooks, and reasoning — not just a diff. Keep it healthy and reasonably priced.
