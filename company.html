DinaBridge is a senior engineering consultancy for production data systems. We connect companies with independent specialists who work directly, without layers, on real production problems — across Elastic, Kafka, Kubernetes, and ScienceLogic.

Why we exist

Production data systems are complex. Finding the right expert is harder.

Most companies running production data infrastructure hit a ceiling. The configuration surface is wide, the failure modes are subtle, and the difference between a system that holds and one that breaks under load is invisible until something goes wrong. Generalist consultants can configure it. Senior specialists can fix it, scale it, and make it work in production. DinaBridge exists to put the right engineer in the room.

What we do

Four practice areas. All senior. Built for production.

Every engagement is scoped, named, and delivered by a senior engineer with hands-on production experience.

Observability

APM instrumentation, distributed tracing, event ingestion pipeline architecture, and observability engineering across Elastic, Kafka-connected telemetry flows, and ScienceLogic SL1.

Search

Relevance tuning, index architecture, cluster performance engineering, and migration planning for enterprise Elasticsearch deployments at scale.

Security & SIEM

SIEM deployment, custom detection rule engineering, and telemetry pipeline implementation for compliance and production threat detection.

Platform & Delivery

Kubernetes cluster engineering, deployment architecture, and infrastructure orchestration for teams running production data systems at scale.
